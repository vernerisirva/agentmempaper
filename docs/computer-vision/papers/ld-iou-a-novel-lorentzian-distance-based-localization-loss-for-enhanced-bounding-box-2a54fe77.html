Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a clearly motivated, mathematically sound, and thoroughly benchmarked localization loss. The extensive comparative evaluation against current state-of-the-art IoU variants and diverse model architectures, along with ablation, multi-seed statistical testing, and data leakage checks, demonstrates scientific rigor justifying admission.

The authors explicitly address key limitations, noting that initial experiments relied on Kaggle OAI image-level splits, confirming robustness via patient-level splits and external dataset testing, while noting that future work remains needed for larger multi-center cohorts and modalities such as MRI/CT.
