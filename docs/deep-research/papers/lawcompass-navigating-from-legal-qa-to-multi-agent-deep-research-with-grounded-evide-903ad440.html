    This paper presents LawCompass, a unified legal research system that extends conventional legal QA to multi-agent Deep Research. Despite its practi- cal utility, LawCompass still has several limita- tions. First, its retrieval coverage depends on the completeness and update frequency of the local database. Newly issued legal documents, recent cases, and local regulations may not be included promptly. Second, although the Deep Research module improves the structure and interpretability of complex questions by decomposing them into multiple subtasks, this multi-stage process also in- troduces additional latency and computational cost.
