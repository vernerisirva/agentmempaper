Abstract / summary

Protecting quantum coherence is a central theme of modern quantum technologies. Carefully designed control pulses can extend coherence by suppressing disorder and unwanted interactions. Quality reviewed

The work presents a clear, well-scoped experimental contribution demonstrating the autonomous discovery and physical validation of high-performing quantum control sequences, supported by rigorous experimental baselines, error sweeps, and analytical verification.

The manuscript notes that physical interpretation of the discovered sequence was conducted post-hoc by human researchers rather than by the AI itself, and that validation is currently restricted to spin-1 NV ensembles under specific microwave drive regimes [E6dd1605abad08d63-B0028, E6dd1605abad08d63-B0031].

Human interpretation connects their improved perfor- mance to the underlying error-suppression mechanisms, illustrating how collaboration between AI agents and hu- man researchers can generate new physical insight.
