    Self-supervised learning in Vision Transformer models, such as DINO, enables robust visual perception without requiring labeled data by leveraging large-scale image datasets. However, these models lack a focusing mechanism during training, resulting in perceptual representations that are often ambiguous or overly similar. In this work, we introduce two visual focusing loss functions designed to establish correspondence between the model's attention and specific regions within images. Specifically, we leverage the multi-head attention mechanism within the model to selectively steer the model's focus, leading to the emergence of spatial attention head specialization, characterized by diverse and spatially focused perceptual capa- bilities without requiring annotations.     A Limitations While our method can train the model to focus on diverse regions within an image, it does not allow explicit control over where each attention head focuses, as the regions of focus are determined implicitly during training. As a result, it is not possible to know in advance which regions each attention head will focus on until after training. Our method also increases the training time and introduces a trade-off between classification performance and performance improvements in zero-shot segmentation, object discovery, and FOIR tasks. Exploring other effective ways to utilize the visual focusing image representations obtained by our method remains an important direction for future work.
