Abstract / summary

Source-only domain generalization (DG) for remote sensing semantic segmentation seeks reliable predictions in unseen domains without target-domain data during training. Quality reviewed

The manuscript provides a well-motivated architectural design addressing known domain shift challenges in remote sensing dense prediction. The experimental design is rigorous, evaluating multiple shift modalities (geographic, spectral, and cross-dataset) with comprehensive baselines, detailed ablation studies, and efficiency profiles.

The work relies on large foundation models (two frozen SAM3 encoders resulting in 1.72B total resident parameters), requiring significant compute resources for inference, and assumes access to pre-defined category text prompts.

Publication: preprint. arXiv manuscript identifier; no external publication established by the available metadata.
