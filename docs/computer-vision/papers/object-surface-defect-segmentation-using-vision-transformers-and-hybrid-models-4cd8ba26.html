Quality pending

The manuscript presents an exploratory comparison but combines disparate benchmarks into an undifferentiated binary pool without dataset-level disaggregation. In addition, critical architectural specifics for the ViT segmentation head are absent, training regimes vary across compared models, and final reported metrics reflect peak validation checkpoint selection rather than held-out test evaluation, generating substantial scientific uncertainty.

The authors acknowledge limitations including hardware intensity, the restriction to binary defect classification, and evaluation confined to controlled benchmark images without real-time latency measurements.
