Abstract / summary

Personalized language agents use persistent memory to adapt to users over time, but the same mechanism creates an attack surface. Quality reviewed

This paper presents an exceptional, rigorous contribution combining theoretical analysis, well-engineered architectural design, and unusually rigorous empirical validation. The inclusion of strong supervision-matched controls, independent human red-teaming, adaptive attacks, and frank reporting of negative/modest transfer results satisfies rigorous curation standards.

Limitations are clearly addressed, including the text-only threat model, remaining vulnerabilities to same-origin and slow gradual attacks, the shrinkage of performance gains when tested on independent external distributions, and increased per-turn inference latency.
