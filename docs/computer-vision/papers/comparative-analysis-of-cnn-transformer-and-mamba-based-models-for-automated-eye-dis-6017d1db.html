    We present a comparative analysis of six models belonging to Convolutional Neu- ral Network (CNN), Transformer, and Mamba families for automated eye disease classification from fundus images under identical experimental conditions. To the best of our knowledge, our work is the first to explore Mamba-based models for auto- mated multi-class eye disease classification from color fundus images. Our analysis suggests that, when evaluated fairly, modern CNN, Transformer, and Mamba-based models provide similar performance for eye disease classification on the Eye Disease Image Dataset (EDID). Along with analyzing individual model performance, we further evaluate performance using a logit-level ensemble of the top three models.
