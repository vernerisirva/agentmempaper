Abstract / summary

Before AI can be trusted to discover new science, it should be able to reproduce what science already knows. Quality reviewed

The benchmark addresses a significant gap in cross-domain scientific execution and reproduction for AI agents, presents a rigorous execution-and-replay verification framework, and provides extensive diagnostics and validation protocols.

The work openly documents its licensing restrictions (three tasks cannot distribute proprietary commercial environments directly), resource ceilings, fallback conventions for refusals, and reliance on an LLM judge for rubrics requiring domain interpretation alongside deterministic checks.
