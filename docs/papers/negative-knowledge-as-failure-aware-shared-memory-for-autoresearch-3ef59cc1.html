Quality reviewed

The paper presents a well-scoped architectural concept backed by a clear implementation schema and solid empirical validation. Deterministic evaluators eliminate subjective scoring bias, and experimental comparisons cleanly isolate the value of structured negative memory against direct retry and raw self-debugging.

The authors transparently discuss constraints, including small sample sizes on PDE benchmarks, negative transfer risks when transferring across systems with incompatible mathematical properties, and the upfront token cost incurred by the curator pass.
