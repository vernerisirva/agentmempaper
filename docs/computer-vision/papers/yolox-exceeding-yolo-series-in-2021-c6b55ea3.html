Abstract / summary

In this report, we present some experienced improvements to YOLO series, forming a new high-performance detector -- YOLOX. Quality reviewed

The work provides clear, scoped empirical advances with transparent ablation studies, consistent hardware benchmarking, and well-supported claims on a standard computer vision benchmark.

The authors explicitly note that higher-capacity Transformer-based detectors and extremely large models (such as YOLOv5-P6) were not explored due to computational constraints, and that the optional end-to-end NMS-free modification incurs a mild drop in AP and latency.

Specifically, OTA [4] analyzes the label assignment from a global perspective and formulate the assigning proce- dure as an Optimal Transport (OT) problem, producing the SOTA performance among the current assigning strate- gies [12, 41, 36, 22, 37].
