Quality reviewed

The empirical findings are supported by disciplined experimental controls (matched visual baselines, spelling-overlap controls, background variation, and text-screened ImageNet and finetuning subsets). The claims are circumspect and fully aligned with the evidence provided.

The authors explicitly acknowledge limitations including lack of evidence for compositionality, the possibility of training data containing co-occurring rendered text (e.g. from web/Instagram data), and that the origin of semantic structure remains an open mechanistic question.

    Can a vision model learn what words mean from pixels alone? We show that the two largest DINOv3 vision transformers organize words rendered in images by word meaning, despite being pretrained using self-supervision on images only, without paired captions, class labels, or other language supervision. We derive word representations from embedding differences between word images and matched controls, then test whether semantically related words are neighbors within each model's embedding space, while controlling for spelling similarity. We find that brand names in the same industry group together, as do nouns from the same semantic domain. Country-capital pairs exhibit consistent vector offsets, similarly to word embeddings trained on text.     The scope and possible sources of semantic knowledge.DINOv3-H and DINOv3-7B exhibit lexical semantic structure connecting written words to one another and to depicted object categories. Not all relations are equally strong: Category membership and country-capital associations are more clearly expressed than synonymy. Similarity and association are distinct (Hill et al., 2015), so factual relationships do not imply equally strong synonym knowledge. These findings do not establish taxonomic hierarchies or sentence-level composition. Nor do consistent offsets improve analogy retrieval over the country-only baseline, reinforcing the distinction between geometric regularity and analogical reasoning (Linzen, 2016).
