Abstract / summary

Patent examination is a challenging real-world task that requires agents to discover sparse prior art, iteratively refine queries, inspect technical evidence, and produce novelty assessments documented in Office Actions (OAs) across heterogeneous scientific and technical domains. Quality reviewed

The work provides a well-scoped, rigorously evaluated contribution to agentic search in a demanding domain. Evaluation metrics are objective and grounded in real-world USPTO examiner citations, method descriptions and ablations are thorough, and claim-evidence alignment is strong.

The authors acknowledge that even top frontier models struggle with prior-art recall (Select-R remaining below 33%), and final decision accuracy correlates only moderately with full examination and retrieval quality.
