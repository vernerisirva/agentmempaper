Quality reviewed

The manuscript offers substantial algorithmic and architectural contributions, clear technical descriptions, rigorous multi-benchmark evaluations with extensive ablations, and candid discussions of limitations.

The authors identify specific failure modes and performance drops, noting that YOLO9000 struggles with object categories such as clothing and equipment where COCO lacks representative bounding box annotations.
