Quality reviewed

The manuscript provides an innovative, conceptually well-grounded, and clearly validated extension to agentic reasoning and applied foresight analysis. The theoretical framework directly motivates the algorithmic components, and the extensive appendices provide complete prompt configurations, algorithmic pseudo-code, and evaluation rubrics.

The authors acknowledge limitations openly, including the relatively modest scale of ADR-bench (15 events) and its heavy reliance on LLM judges for rubric-based scoring.
