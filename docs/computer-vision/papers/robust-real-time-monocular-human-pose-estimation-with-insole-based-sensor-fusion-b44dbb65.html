Real-time monocular human pose estimation is an important sensing component for human-centered intelligent systems. Quality reviewed

The study demonstrates high scientific quality with a clear, technically sound method, a well-synchronized novel dataset, solid comparisons against fair vision-only temporal baselines, thorough ablations, and objective ground-truth evaluation using optical/inertial MoCap reference tracking.

The authors clearly acknowledge key limitations, including the reliance on root-relative skeletons rather than long-term absolute global trajectories due to lack of absolute global yaw reference, the need for wider demographic and activity testing, and remaining validation requirements in downstream clinical or ergonomic tasks.
