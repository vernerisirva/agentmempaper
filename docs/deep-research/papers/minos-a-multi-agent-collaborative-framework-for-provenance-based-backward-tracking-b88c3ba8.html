Abstract / summary

Sophisticated cyber attacks, particularly Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs), require effective post-intrusion forensic analysis. Provenance-based backward tracking reconstructs attack scenarios by tracing causality from security alerts, but existing methods rely on low-level statistical features and rigid traversal...

Quality reviewed

The manuscript addresses a well-defined security challenge (dependency explosion and semantic ambiguity in provenance tracking) with a well-engineered multi-agent LLM solution, comprehensive empirical evidence across multiple benchmarks, and rigorous ablation experiments.

The authors acknowledge higher inference latency compared to traditional statistical methods, dependencies on POI alert quality, and data privacy implications when utilizing hosted LLMs on sensitive audit logs.
