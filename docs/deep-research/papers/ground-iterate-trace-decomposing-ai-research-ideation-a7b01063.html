AI assessment inconclusive

While thoroughly executed and transparently reported, the core methodological innovation—validated explicit creative operators—fails to establish a statistically significant performance benefit over simple free-form iteration in the ten-condition ablation study.

The manuscript candidly acknowledges that traces demonstrate procedural history rather than causal mechanisms, that human validation of idea quality was not conducted on newly generated ideas, and that operator ablations showed no statistically significant quality advantage after correction.

Each operator call is checked against structural acceptance conditions and recorded, producing a trace of intermediate transformations and declared lit- erature sources.
