Quality reviewed

The manuscript provides a methodologically rigorous, highly self-critical evaluation benchmark. The experiments systematically address threats to validity, such as padding attacks, deletion responses, and reference-selection bias, and transparently document performance across multiple LLM judges and human audit samples.

The authors thoroughly discuss limitations, including the lack of external expert validation beyond the first-author audit, the domain/language focus on Chinese financial reports, the potential sensitivity to prompt design or model families, and the residual unmeasured effects of web browsing randomness.
