    The AI scientist in its new, software sense is no longer a thought experiment. It writes papers that pass review, and with enough compute and a good formal checker it can settle problems that resisted people for generations. It is also much better at carry ing out research than at choosing it, and better at producing answers than understanding. After more than twenty years of watching the boundary move between what the researcher does and what the machine does, one finds that this time the boundary has moved to the part of the job that was always the most important: asking the right question and knowing why the answer is right.
