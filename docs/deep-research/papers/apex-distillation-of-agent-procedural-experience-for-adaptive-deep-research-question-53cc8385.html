    Deep research agents augment large language models with external tools to answer com- plex, long-horizon questions through multi- turn reasoning. Learning from prior experi- ence is crucial for continual improvement, yet existing methods either retrieve verbose task- specific traces that burden decision-making, or distill procedural skills that remain decou- pled from downstream policy adaptation. We propose APEX, a hierarchical experience uti- lization framework that organizes interaction history into instance-level trajectory memories and category-level procedural skills, and cou- ples them through a closed-loop architecture of Executor, Distiller, and Planner. The three modules are optimized via a three-stage al- ternating GRPO training paradigm, enabling reward-guided skill distillation rather than fixed-prompt generation.     Training Settings:Our training framework is built on veRL (Sheng et al., 2025) and follows a three-stage alternating optimization paradigm.
