Abstract / summary

Quantum error correction (QEC) is indispensable for scalable fault-tolerant quantum computing. However, discovering QEC codes that remain effective is challenging, as logical performance depends on the interplay between code structure, hardware, syndrome extraction, and decoding, which often impose competing require...

Quality reviewed

The study provides a well-controlled, significant contribution to automated quantum error-correcting code design. The claims are fully substantiated by comprehensive circuit simulations, robust baselines, and ablation experiments across multiple LLM engines.

The manuscript transparently discusses computational bottlenecks in circuit simulation and decoder evaluation, reliance on depolarizing noise models, and the need to scale beyond memory experiments to full logical Clifford operations and diverse physical architectures.
