Quality reviewed

The paper presents a coherent, mathematically complete framework with clear conceptual positioning against both classical utility models and quantum decision approaches, supported by solid quantitative validations on real empirical data and detailed dynamic simulations.

The manuscript focuses on mean-field all-to-all communication topologies without spatial graph constraints and tests dynamic properties predominantly on binary choice setups like the Allais paradox.
