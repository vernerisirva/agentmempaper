Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a rigorous benchmark for deep research agents with thorough documentation of dataset curation, rubric design, judge calibration against human ground truth, and extensive cross-model evaluation.

The authors identify model-level failure modes (premature termination, temporal violations, missing details) and report the evaluation window constraints.

    We adopt a rubric-based LLM-as-a-Judge approach to assess each final report. The resulting rubric- level judgments are aggregated into a score. Given a task, its rubric set, and a candidate report, an LLM judge assigns a binary Pass/Fail label to each rubric. Judgments follow the rubric's acceptance conditions, including factual targets, source requirements, and temporal scope. Semantically equivalent wording and numerical approximations are accepted where permitted by the rubric. Let Rq denote the rubric set for task q, and let yq,r ∈ {0,1}indicate whether the candidate report for taskqsatisfies rubricr. For each rubric r in task q, we use its annotator-assigned weight wq,r, which reflects relative importance within the task.
