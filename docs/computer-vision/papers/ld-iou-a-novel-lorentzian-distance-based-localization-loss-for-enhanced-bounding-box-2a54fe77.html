Quality reviewed

The study presents a clear, well-scoped methodological advancement with transparent loss function equations and rigorous empirical validation. The experimental protocol ensures proper baselines with fixed hyperparameters, addresses potential leakage via patient-level splits, and verifies generalizability across multiple detector architectures and an external dataset.

The authors acknowledge limitations including primary validation on single-modality X-ray datasets from OAI, the need for evaluation on larger patient-independent cohorts across multi-center clinical settings, and testing on other imaging modalities such as MRI or CT.
