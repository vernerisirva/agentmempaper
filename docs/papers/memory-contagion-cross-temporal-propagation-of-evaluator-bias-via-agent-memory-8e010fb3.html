AI assessment inconclusive

Given that the phenomenon completely fails to replicate for authority bias under controlled conditions and does not manifest in newer or different model families for length bias, evidence for Memory Contagion as a general property of LLM agent memory architectures remains weak and uncertain.

The authors explicitly disclose that authority bias failed to propagate in all 15 controlled runs, that newer models show zero contagion, and that additive separability and rejection-sampling bias injection may introduce confounds.
