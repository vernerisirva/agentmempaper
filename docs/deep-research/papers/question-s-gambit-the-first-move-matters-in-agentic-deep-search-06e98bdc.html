Quality reviewed

The paper provides an intuitive, well-engineered architectural enhancement to deep-search agents with clear motivation, transparent experimental methodology, strong empirical gains across diverse models, and comprehensive statistical and error analyses.

The authors clearly delineate limitations, acknowledging the focus on fixed corpora rather than live web search, the reliance on BM25 for candidate generation, potential anchoring risks on misleading seed documents, and the need for adaptive mechanisms to determine when to trigger the gambit.
