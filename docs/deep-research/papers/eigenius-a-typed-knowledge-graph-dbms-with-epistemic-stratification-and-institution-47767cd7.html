Abstract / summary

As "AI Scientists" emerge to drive research via the Model Context Protocol (MCP), systems relying on ephemeral scripts will fail. Quality reviewed

The manuscript makes a well-articulated, substantive systems and formal-methods contribution. The design choices are thoroughly justified, the implementation is evaluated across both synthetic scale benchmarks and a complex real-world scientific workflow, and theoretical boundaries and ongoing work are explicitly demarcated.

The authors transparently note limitations, including shifting from Wasm sandboxing to container isolation, an open strong-normalization proof for full kernel termination, the uncharacterized status of general pushouts across conflicting branches, and unclosed autonomous loops for AI agents.
