Abstract / summary

AI research agents increasingly support ideation, literature search, coding, experimental execution, analysis, and manuscript drafting across the scientific workflow. This progress advances automated discovery, but workflow automation is not scientific cognition. Scientific reasoning is cumulative and path-dependent...
