Screening rationale Studies model-integrated conditional memory; matched title/abstract rules: conditional-memory.

Scholarly quality evidence

The paper presents a clear and well-motivated methodological contribution combining conditional memory and sparse expert routing for efficient protein language modeling. The architecture is described with sufficient detail, and the initialization strategies for both modules are designed to preserve the pretrained function. The evaluation covers multiple benchmarks, including ProteinGym, FLIP, and contact prediction, with ablation studies supporting the complementary contributions of the two modules. The biological specialization analyses provide additional evidence for the interpretability and functional relevance of the learned mechanisms. The paper is transparent about limitations and includes detailed implementation and evaluation protocols. The main weaknesses are the lack of a fully controlled ablation for the data sampling strategy and the relatively modest absolute gains over the ESM2-150M baseline, which the authors acknowledge.

Screening rationale Studies model-integrated conditional memory; matched title/abstract rules: hashed-ngram-memory, memory-transfer.

Scholarly quality evidence

This is a well-executed empirical study of cross-model frozen-memory transfer. The contribution is clearly formulated as an operational test of whether external memory remains useful after source backbone removal. The methodology is rigorous, with controlled ablations isolating memory content, addressing integrity, and reader design. The evaluation is extensive, spanning multiple model families, tokenizers, scales, and downstream tasks, with appropriate controls including permuted keys, random memory, and parameter-matched FFN baselines. The central claim that reader design is first-order is well supported by the dual-layer and multi-branch ablation results. The paper also demonstrates intellectual honesty by reporting negative results on TruthfulQA and acknowledging that transfer primarily improves initialization rather than asymptotic ceiling. Limitations are explicitly discussed. The main weaknesses are minor presentation issues in the appendix, including apparent internal notes or incomplete edits, and some informal phrasing. Overall, the evidence chain is strong and the conclusions are appropriately scoped.
