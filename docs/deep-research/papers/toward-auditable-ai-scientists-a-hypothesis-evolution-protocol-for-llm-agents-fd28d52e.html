Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents an inventive, soundly implemented framework for scientific LLM auditing with solid experimental controls, clear baselines, and honest discussion of boundaries, meeting library standards for a pass.

The authors explicitly acknowledge that evidence validity currently relies on single-agent self-assessment, that the discovered governing rules reflect the behavior of a fixed MLIP rather than DFT or experimental physical reality, and that weaker LLMs struggle to manage complex hypothesis lineages [E269bb07cb3e53c1a-B0026, E269bb07cb3e53c1a-B0029, E269bb07cb3e53c1a-B0032].
