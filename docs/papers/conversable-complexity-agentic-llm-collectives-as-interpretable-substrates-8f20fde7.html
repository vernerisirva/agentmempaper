Quality reviewed

The paper provides a well-reasoned, clearly articulated conceptual contribution that establishes clear criteria and taxonomies for using agentic LLMs within Artificial Life, supported by rigorous synthesis and appropriate treatment of epistemic caveats.

The authors explicitly discuss key constraints, including that natural-language legibility does not guarantee truthfulness due to phenomena like sycophancy, hidden reasoning, and alignment faking, requiring corroboration from non-verbal interpretability channels.
