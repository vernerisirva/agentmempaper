Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a thorough, well-controlled experimental framework featuring multi-task evaluations, rigorous isolation, and clear ablation comparisons between baseline, naive memory, and scientific evolution. Claims are appropriately scoped and corroborated by empirical measurements.

The transfer of dynamic voltage prediction is validated across unseen temperatures within known driving cycle profiles rather than completely unseen duty cycles, and certain complex failure modes remain unresolved across challenging physical tasks.

Exploration traces make updates to knowledge, skills, and multi-agent orchestration.
