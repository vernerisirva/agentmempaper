    Deep research agents are increasingly used to produce long-form financial reports, yet large-scale evaluation remains bottlenecked by the need for human experts to define and execute high-quality rubrics. We address this problem by proposing a scalable pipeline for generating high-quality rubrics without human experts in the final loop.     Consistency alone is insufficient: a rubric may be perfectly stable while still assigning the same effec- tive judgment to all products. We therefore apply a second filter at the cross-report level. For each consistency-passed rubric, we compute the major- ity label for each of the 10 product reports. A rubric is considered distinguishable if at least one report receives a majority-yes label and at least one re- port receives a majority-no label. This removes one-sided rubrics such as always-yes or always-no criteria that are stable but uninformative for product comparison.
