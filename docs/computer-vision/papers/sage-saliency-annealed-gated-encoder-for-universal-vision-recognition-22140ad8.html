Abstract / summary

When adapting self-supervised Vision Transformers (ViTs) to dense prediction, existing methods often treat the ViT as a passive feature extractor and may underuse the rich priors encoded in its representations, including saliency, global context, and high-frequency details. Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a scoped architectural and training innovation with precise mathematical descriptions, reproducible implementation details, rigorous ablations, and extensive cross-task empirical validation using objective standard benchmarks.

The authors transparently note that baseline models vary in pretraining corpora (O365 vs IN-22K/IN-1K) and detector parameter counts, preventing isolated attribution of all performance margins exclusively to the backbone architecture.
