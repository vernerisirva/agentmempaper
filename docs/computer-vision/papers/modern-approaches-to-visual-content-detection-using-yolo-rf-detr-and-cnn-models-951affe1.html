Quality pending

While the paper provides a straightforward comparative study on a specialized destructive visual content dataset, the technical contributions are limited to dataset assembly and standard fine-tuning of existing models. Given ambiguities around augmentation pipeline splitting, single-run evaluation without error bounds, and limited algorithmic novelty, the study's scientific rigor remains uncertain.

The authors acknowledge class imbalance and limited sample sizes for specific extremist symbols, but omit discussion of potential data leakage during augmentation, lack of repeated trials, and the absence of external benchmark evaluations.
