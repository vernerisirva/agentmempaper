Quality reviewed

The paper provides an insightful, rigorous, and clearly scoped contribution to the evaluation of AI agents in science, exposing how outcome-level metrics fail to capture flawed mechanistic justifications and demonstrating an effective cross-regime audit check.

The authors transparently discuss key limitations, including reliance on a single particle-physics simulation domain, small sample sizes (N=5 per prompt; N=1 for cross-model probes), single-author post-hoc coding, and focus on single-agent loops rather than multi-agent systems [Eeebd3aad7c17b63e-B0051, Eeebd3aad7c17b63e-B0052, Eeebd3aad7c17b63e-B0053].
