Quality reviewed

This work sets a high standard for benchmarking post-federation transfer by combining theoretical grounding with scrupulously controlled experiments, pre-registered chronologies, and explicit disclosures of both positive and negative outcomes. It clearly qualifies for acceptance.

The manuscript transparently discusses key constraints, including small test cohorts for MELD/TCGA, simulated action silos, in-domain public anchors, lack of formal differential privacy guarantees, and the non-equivalence of finite sample guarantees to prospective individual safety.
