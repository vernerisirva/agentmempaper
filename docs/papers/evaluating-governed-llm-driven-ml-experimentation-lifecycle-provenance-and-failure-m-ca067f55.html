Quality reviewed

The paper provides an exceptionally rigorous, well-engineered, and transparently characterized framework for auditing autonomous ML experimentation agents. By focusing on governance, auditability, and failure classification rather than merely reporting peak task scores, it makes a substantial methodological contribution supported by solid empirical evidence and reproducible open artifacts.

The manuscript explicitly details the descriptive nature of memory ablation orderings across tasks, reset protocol anomalies in earlier runs, the unverified root cause of total worker failure on the autoresearch node, and the absence of a multi-rater human audit study [Ea71b3056d68a691d-B0070, Ea71b3056d68a691d-B0074].
