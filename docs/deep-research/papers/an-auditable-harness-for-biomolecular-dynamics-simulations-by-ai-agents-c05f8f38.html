Abstract / summary

AI agents now generate biological hypotheses faster than they can be tested. Hypotheses stated at the atomic level can be examined with molecular dynamics (MD) simulations. For new questions without a reference, trust in an agent-run simulation rests on a tool-written process record, not the agent's account. Existin...

Quality reviewed

The study provides a thoroughly documented, open-source tool with rigorous methodology and well-controlled evaluations on independent external datasets. The ablation and multi-model experimental design strongly validate the necessity of the proposed harness architecture.

The authors clearly acknowledge that DAG execution guarantees do not overcome conformational sampling limitations, that the harness cannot autonomously evaluate whether physical force-field choices suit arbitrary scientific problems, and that the DAG requires write-protection to prevent unauthorized modification by agents.
