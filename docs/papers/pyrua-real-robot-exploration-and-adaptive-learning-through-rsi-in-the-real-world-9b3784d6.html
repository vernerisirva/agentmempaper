AI assessment inconclusive

While the empirical exploration of coding agents in physical robotics is detailed and offers valuable qualitative insights, the experimental rigor is undermined by reporting development-stage cumulative success rather than isolated held-out evaluations of final policies, along with unmatched seed baselines in simulation benchmarks. Given these methodological and evaluation uncertainties, an uncertain verdict is appropriate.

The authors transparently report limitations, including that offline programs can fail on unseen variations, contact-rich tasks often require online intervention, and human guidance was used during exploration deadlocks.
