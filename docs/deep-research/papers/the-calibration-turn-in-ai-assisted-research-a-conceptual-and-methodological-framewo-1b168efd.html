It may be decomposed as: SupD,V (E, C) = FD,V (d, i, r, e, cid, ξ, n), (34) where d is directness of the test, i is independence from the generating system, r is reproducibility, e is effect size, cid is causal identification, ξ is external validity, and n captures negative evidence, failed cases, or counterexamples.
