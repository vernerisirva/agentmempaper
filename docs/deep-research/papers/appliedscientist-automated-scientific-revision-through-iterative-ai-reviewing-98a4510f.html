Quality reviewed

The paper makes a substantive, clearly scoped contribution with a well-described system, appropriate controls, independent external evaluation, and a nuanced characterization of what iterative revision can and cannot improve. The main weaknesses are acknowledged and bounded by the study design, and the evidence supports the stated claims without overclaiming.

The evaluation set is small (30 papers) and constrained to ICLR papers reproducible on a single 96 GB GPU, with cost (~$65/paper) cited as the limiting factor. The same reviewer both guides and scores the reviewer-driven conditions, though the external Stanford Reviewer evaluation partially mitigates this circularity; Stanford Reviewer is not applied to the AI-initialized or self-revision conditions.
