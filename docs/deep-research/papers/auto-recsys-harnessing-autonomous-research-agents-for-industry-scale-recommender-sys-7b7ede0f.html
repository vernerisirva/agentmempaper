Quality reviewed

Codex evidence audit of the saved DeepSeek proposed pass, following the reviewed literal line-wrap correction. Auto-RecSys presents a well-motivated systems contribution for autonomous experimentation on industry-scale recommendation models. The architecture is clearly described, and the evaluation provides quantitative evidence of improved execution reliability and qualitative evidence of robust recovery. However, the evaluation is limited in scale and lacks statistical rigor, and the scientific value of generated ideas is not directly validated. Overall, the paper is a credible systems contribution with clear limitations.
