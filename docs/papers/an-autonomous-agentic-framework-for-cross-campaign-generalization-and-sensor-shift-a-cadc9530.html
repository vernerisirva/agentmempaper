Quality reviewed

The work presents a thorough architectural design, clear mathematical formulation of the memory graph, robust isolation protocols to prevent data leakage during autonomous optimization, and meaningful empirical comparisons and ablations across multiple campaigns and hardware setups.

The authors explicitly report substantial GPU computation requirements (91 GPU-hours for SERS), acknowledge validation primarily spanning 1D-to-2D spectral/temporal modalities, and clarify that aged-sensor results serve as a temporal proof-of-concept rather than universal cross-instrument calibration.
