AI assessment inconclusive

While the authors provide commendable transparency about what is implemented versus proposed, the paper's primary conceptual contributions (cross-expert attribution and cold-expert resolution) are entirely unexecuted and unevaluated. Given the reliance on single-model LLM judging on an offline benchmark without validating the titular distillation mechanism, scientific quality warrants an uncertain decision.

The manuscript candidly acknowledges critical limitations: the attribution extension is not implemented or evaluated, the primary evaluation relies on a single LLM judge without human ground-truth verification, and supplementary customs evaluations lack matched baselines.

Publication: preprint. arXiv manuscript identifier; no external publication established by the available metadata.
