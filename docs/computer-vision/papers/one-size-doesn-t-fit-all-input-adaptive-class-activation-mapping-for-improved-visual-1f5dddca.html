Quality reviewed

The work presents an adequately scoped, well-formalized post-hoc explainability method backed by rigorous empirical benchmarks on standard vision datasets and models, extensive ablation studies, and clear reporting of methodological boundaries.

The authors document the increased computational footprint from second-order Hessian backward passes, higher variance on certain CNN curvature distributions, and less compact localization on complex multi-object or cluttered scenes.
