While the representation and questions are intriguing, testing 10 runs of a single prompt on a single black-box agent constitutes preliminary or pilot-level evidence rather than a robust, generalizable benchmark study. The dependence on unvalidated LLM extraction and deduplication without human annotation audits further introduces scientific uncertainty.

The authors explicitly acknowledge major limitations, including reliance on a single prompt, domain, and commercial system, inability to separate retrieval from generation stochasticity, potential web drift, exact URL matching constraints, and un-audited extraction/canonicalization noise [Ea9a6f0d489b70193-B0022, Ea9a6f0d489b70193-B0024].
