Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a rigorous empirical study that systematically unifies and benchmarks practical object detection optimizations. The methodology is clearly defined, the ablations are comprehensive, and performance claims are backed by standardized MS COCO metrics and multi-platform latency tests.

The work focuses on standard anchor-based 2D detection on GPUs, acknowledging that models optimal for classification do not inherently transfer optimality to detection [E055c2b905f487af7-B0048, E055c2b905f487af7-B0049]. Discussion of memory-constrained training settings is addressed via CmBN and mini-batch ablations [E055c2b905f487af7-B0050].
