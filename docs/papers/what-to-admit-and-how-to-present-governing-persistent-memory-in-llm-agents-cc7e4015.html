Abstract / summary

Quality reviewed

This study provides a rigorous, preregistered experimental investigation that cleanly dissects admission from presentation in agent memory. Its claims are exceptionally well-disciplined, negative and bounded results are prominently analyzed, and statistical adjustments are appropriately applied throughout.

The authors document clear boundaries, including increased missed-personalization failures, lack of improvement on GPT-OSS-120B under tested configurations, failure to mitigate memory–evidence conflicts when conflicting memory is present, and reliance on an automated external judge with human validation limited to the development set [E9c43c60ccb5c03a1-B0011, E9c43c60ccb5c03a1-B0031, E9c43c60ccb5c03a1-B0051, E9c43c60ccb5c03a1-B0056].
