Abstract / summary

Retrieval systems help deep research agents generate high-quality answers by providing relevant documents. However, existing retrievers typically select documents through relevance matching, while individually well-matched top-$k$ documents may not form a \textit{set} that satisfies the complex information needs of...

Quality reviewed

The work provides a well-defined and rigorously evaluated approach to set-based reranking for iterative agentic search. The experiments include competitive baselines, clear ablations demonstrating the utility of both SFT and RL stages, and robust external corroboration via objective exact-match RAG evaluations.

The authors transparently discuss dependency on costly proprietary LLMs for rubric scoring during training, the evaluation of sampled query subsets for deep research, and confounding downstream agent generation errors.
