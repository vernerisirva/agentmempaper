Abstract / summary

RiO-DETR is the first real-time end-to-end transformer for oriented object detection, yet further localization gains require a more expressive box representation. Although distributional regression has succeeded in horizontal DETRs, extending it to oriented detection introduces geometry-misaligned boundary modeling...

Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a sound, well-motivated architectural extension to real-time oriented DETRs with thorough mathematical descriptions, clear ablation studies, and robust benchmarking on standard datasets without methodological circularity.

The authors explicitly analyze precision-recall trade-offs across varying thresholds, the impact of confidence calibration on AP50, and representation ambiguities (canonical-axis exchange) for near-square objects.
