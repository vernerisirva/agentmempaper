Abstract / summary

Natural face-to-face human--robot interaction requires a robot to understand an evolving social situation, decide when to engage, and express its intent through coordinated physical behavior. The contribution is substantive and well-scoped, providing an end-to-end operational robotics framework validated with physical embodiment, controlled human subject experiments, and timing benchmarks.

The authors explicitly acknowledge limitations including residual multi-second latency stemming from cascaded VLM and facial generation models that preclude sub-second conversational turn-taking, as well as hardware constraints in mechanical facial expressiveness.
