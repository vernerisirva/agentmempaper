AI assessment inconclusive

While the experimental logging, reproducibility framework, and candor regarding protocol deviations are commendable, the paper's substantive evaluation of scientific quality rests entirely on uncalibrated external LLM reviewers without human expert corroboration across only five runs. Under conservative curation standards, the lack of independent ground truth or human calibration for paper quality warrants an uncertain verdict.

The authors explicitly disclose protocol deviations (post-hoc counting of the pilot run), sample size limits (five runs, single research model family), lack of human evaluation, and the tendency of agents to converge on narrow open-data resources.
