Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a rigorous, well-engineered architectural design addressing a vital open challenge in deploying autonomous/agentic AI within privacy-regulated biomedical environments. The system is validated through extensive real-world clinical data with clear controls, human-expert comparative baselines, and transparent documentation of limitations and failure recovery.

The authors transparently acknowledge that evaluation is largely focused on a single-institution deployment, GWAS results reflect locus replication rather than novel variant discovery without per-SNP effect extraction, and the secondary drug-induced liver injury study relied on a modest compound sample size.
