Quality reviewed

The manuscript provides a rigorously constructed surgical dataset addressing a prominent methodological issue in surgical novel view synthesis (trajectory overfitting) and supports its claims with extensive benchmarking and thorough technical ablations.

The authors document key limitations including hand-guided motion in ex vivo sessions, the requirement of marker inpainting for dynamic sequences, and baseline/trocar field-of-view constraints inherent to surgical robotics.

Publication: preprint. arXiv manuscript identifier; no external publication established by the available metadata.
