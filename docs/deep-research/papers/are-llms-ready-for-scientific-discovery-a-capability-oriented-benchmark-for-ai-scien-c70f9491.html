Quality reviewed

The benchmark provides a well-substantiated, methodologically sound framework addressing known pitfalls in execution-only LLM evaluations. Data generation, programmatic verification, and diagnostic error evaluations are thoroughly documented and scientifically credible.

The authors explicitly discuss constraints including the potential gap between synthetic semantic graph dynamics and real experimental confounders, the risk of MCQ options inducing superficial heuristics, and annotating only the first execution error.
