The contribution is modest, essentially consisting of fine-tuning an off-the-shelf ViT-small model on three legacy datasets. The gap between the formulated squeeze-fusion method and the actually evaluated architecture, combined with the lack of direct baseline comparisons and missing cloud/edge empirical runtime evaluations despite the paper's central title and theme, warrants an uncertain assessment.
