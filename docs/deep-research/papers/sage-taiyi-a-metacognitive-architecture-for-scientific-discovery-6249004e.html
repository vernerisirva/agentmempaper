    Following Nelson and Narens' account of the meta level as maintaining a dynamic model of object- level cognition [9], the Cognitive State models the current search. It contains two kinds of content. The first is a selective, source-linked representation of object-level scientific understanding, retain- ing key findings, competing explanations, necessary assumptions, applicability conditions, and unre- solved questions. The second consists of monitoring-derived assessments of the evidential status of key judgments, unresolved conflicts and uncertainties, and dependencies shared across ideas. The former selectively represents what the system currently understands about the scientific problem; the latter assesses how this understanding should guide further search.
