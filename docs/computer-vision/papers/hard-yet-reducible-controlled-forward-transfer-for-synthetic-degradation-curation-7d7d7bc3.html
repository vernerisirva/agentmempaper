Abstract / summary

Selecting synthetic degradations for dense prediction requires an estimate of their training utility, the generalization gain they bring under a finite training budget. Clean and degraded twins share content and labels, suggesting a score based on how much short training reduces the excess error caused by degradatio...

Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a rigorous, well-controlled experimental setup addressing a genuine confound in synthetic data selection. The mathematical formulation is clear, baselines are fairly adapted under matched budgets, and empirical analyses thoroughly probe underlying mechanisms.

The authors transparently document limitations, including reliance on single-run evaluations for final training, residual drift near gate thresholds, reliance on cross-family transfer for completely novel degradation types, and probe budget heuristics.
