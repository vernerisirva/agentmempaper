Quality reviewed

The work makes a clear, well-scoped architectural and empirical contribution to autonomous scientific agents. The system implementation is thoroughly documented, experimental baselines use controlled environments and identical backbones, and the headline metrics are backed by official benchmark judges as well as independent blinded evaluations.

The authors explicitly note that the tight component coupling precludes traditional single-component ablations and that the case trace did not trigger rollback or cross-run retrieval [Eca2141e53543d7d6-B0036, Eca2141e53543d7d6-B0167]. Limitations in task alignment compared to specialized baselines are acknowledged [Eca2141e53543d7d6-B0032].
