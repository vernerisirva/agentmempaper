Quality reviewed

The paper provides an extensive and well-controlled study demonstrating end-to-end multi-agent orchestration of physical laboratory instrumentation. The claims are carefully bounded, ablations clearly delineate the role of Socratic midwifery, and reported physical outcomes are verified through objective numerical benchmarks.

The authors explicitly discuss key limitations, noting that Socratic interactions remain in natural language without formal logic representations, human supervision remains necessary as a safety gate, and repetitive large-scale data collection was offloaded to human operators [E30a68f4bdbf83274-B0042, E30a68f4bdbf83274-B0068, E30a68f4bdbf83274-B0069].
