Abstract / summary

The kissing-number problem is a classical problem in discrete geometry whose exact solution is known in only a few dimensions. The bounds are constructive lower bounds rather than exact kissing numbers (except where known upper bounds match), and the local optimizations are restricted to the explored structured models and witness pools.
