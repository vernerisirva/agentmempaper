The unexplained pairs suggest theexistence of missing idea factors that separate the programs in each pair. For the selected pairs, a separate proposer LLM reads the programs to propose candidate idea factors that distinguish them. Each candidate is then annotated on the whole archive, and is admitted into the idea memory if each of its values is supported by enough programs and its values separate the scores.     of each action (Appendix H.3). Exploration at plateaus.Oftentimes, evolutionary discovery can concentrate around its best programs and a single strategy. Consequently, the ideas widely used in top-scoring programs stop varying, and their roles cannot be identified from the archive (Thm. 3.3).
