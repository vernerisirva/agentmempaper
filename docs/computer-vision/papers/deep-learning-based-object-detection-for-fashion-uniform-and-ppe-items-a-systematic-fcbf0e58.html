    There are some limitations to this review that need to be acknowledged. Firstly, database search was carried out using only one database – Scopus. While Scopus offers broad coverage of computer vision and deep learning literature due to its inclusion of peer- reviewed publications from IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer and Elsevier, it does not include arXiv, and relevant contributions in the preprint stage could not be included in this review. This is especially pertinent for computer vision, since the field favours publishing in conferences and preprint platforms such as arXiv. Second, the search was limited to articles and conference papers published in English only and between 2021 and 2026, so that important foundational work published prior to 2021 was not synthesised in this review, though it was referred to in the introduction.
