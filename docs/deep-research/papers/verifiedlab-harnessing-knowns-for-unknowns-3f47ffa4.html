Abstract / summary

Across human history, knowledge has undergone several transitions, from orality to writing and then to digital archives. Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a comprehensive, well-controlled study introducing an active verification paradigm for scientific literature extraction. It combines expert-annotated calibration, rigorous temporal holdout validation, and transparent reporting of failure modes and boundaries.

The authors clearly acknowledge limitations, including potential self-preference bias when LLM evaluators share model families, the restriction of the schema to champion devices in one material domain, and the lower reliability of complex procedural fields like stability [E030f9356cb95c4f5-B0048, E030f9356cb95c4f5-B0049].
