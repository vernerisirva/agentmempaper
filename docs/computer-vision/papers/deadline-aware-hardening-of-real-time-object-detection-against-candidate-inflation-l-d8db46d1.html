Quality reviewed

This paper presents a methodologically thorough, nuanced, and self-critical systems investigation of adversarial latency in object detection pipelines. The experimental design is well-controlled, artifacts and provenance are documented, and claims strictly adhere to empirical findings without overreach.

The authors explicitly detail key limitations, noting that their algorithmic bound does not guarantee worst-case execution time (WCET), depends on session sample counts for tail quantile estimation, and cannot protect upstream pipeline stages like image decoding from format-induced overheads.
