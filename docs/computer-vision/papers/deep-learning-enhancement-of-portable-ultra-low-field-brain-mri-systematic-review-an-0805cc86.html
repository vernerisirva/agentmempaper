Abstract / summary

Retrospectively registered protocol of a systematic review and meta-analysis of deep learning (DL) enhancement of portable ultra-low-field (ULF, ≤0.1 T) brain MRI. The review asks whether DL-enhanced ULF images agree better with same-subject high-field (≥1.5 T) MRI than standard ULF images, and whether DL adds value...
