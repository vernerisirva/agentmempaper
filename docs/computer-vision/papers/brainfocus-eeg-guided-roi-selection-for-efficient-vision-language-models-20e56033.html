AI assessment inconclusive

While the framework and efficiency metrics are systematically presented, the study's construct validity is undermined by synthetic category-level pairing of EEG data with artificial cluttered images, as well as reliance on uncalibrated LLM evaluation for open-ended VQA answers. Consequently, scientific certainty regarding real EEG-guided efficiency benefits remains unestablished.

The authors acknowledge domain differences between real and generated images and declining coverage at higher class counts, but fail to address the core artificiality of category-level EEG-image matching versus live perceptual EEG routing.

Electroencephalography (EEG) signals, which capture human neural responses to visual stimuli, can provide a human-derived semantic cue about the region of interest (ROI). However, EEG-guided visual category decoding remains imperfect, making direct ROI routing unreliable. In this work, we propose BrainFocus, a reliable EEG-guided efficient VLM framework for VQA.
