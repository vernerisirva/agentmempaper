Quality reviewed

This study addresses an important gap in understanding the robustness of autonomous research agents under realistic, imperfect user prompts. The experimental design is rigorous, methodologically sound, transparently scoped, and thoroughly validated.

The authors explicitly acknowledge limitations including a predominant focus on English queries, evaluation restricted to agents with accessible intermediate trajectories, and lack of coverage for proprietary closed-source systems without exposed traces.

    Deep research agents (DRAs) conduct long- horizon reasoning, search, and literature syn- thesis to provide long-form answers to com- plex, domain-specific questions. These agents are typically trained and evaluated on fully specified user queries. However, real-world user queries can often be underspecified. We introduce UNDERSPEC-DR, a framework for systematically evaluating how underspecified queries affect agents' final answer quality and intermediate search behavior. Across three DRAs we evaluated, underspecification sub- stantially reduces how well DRA's answers fulfill users' information needs, even though they remain grounded in cited evidence.
