AI assessment inconclusive

While the architectural concepts for lightweight detection are reasonable, augmenting the raw images from 1,000 to 4,000 before splitting them 8:1:1 into train, validation, and test sets introduces near-duplicate leakage into the test set. This creates high scientific uncertainty regarding whether the reported performance reflects genuine generalization or memorization of augmented pairs.

The authors acknowledge sensitivity to complex environmental conditions and illumination changes, but fail to address the methodological flaw of augmenting the dataset prior to random train/val/test partitioning.
