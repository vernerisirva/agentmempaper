VI. DISCUSSION ANDRECOMMENDATIONS Two conclusions follow that a specification sheet would not have predicted. First, the B300 should be purchased for what it is: a part optimized for large-GEMM, low-precision transformer work with a large weight footprint. It is the better choice for ViT- class inference and training, where it wins on both throughput and energy efficiency, and for workloads gated on per-device capacity. It is the worse choice for convolutional networks, for FP64 codes, and — at present — for 70B LLM serving.
