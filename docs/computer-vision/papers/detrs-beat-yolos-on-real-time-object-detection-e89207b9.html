Abstract / summary

The YOLO series has become the most popular framework for real-time object detection due to its reasonable trade-off between speed and accuracy. Quality reviewed

The study provides clear architectural innovations, transparent experimental controls, comprehensive comparisons against SOTA detectors on a standard benchmark, and detailed ablations validating the hybrid encoder and query selection mechanisms.

The authors explicitly disclose that RT-DETR lags slightly behind strong YOLO baselines (e.g., YOLOv8-L and YOLOv7-X) on small object detection AP (APS).
