Quality reviewed

The manuscript presents a scoped architectural design with complete implementation configurations, rigorous comparative testing across multiple standard benchmark datasets, paired statistical tests, and explicit ablation verification.

The authors transparently discuss remaining limitations, such as sensitivity to extreme compression and motion blur, the absence of confidence calibration analysis, and untested generalization to diffusion-based video generation models.
