Abstract / summary

Long-running language-model agents depend on persistent memory. Many agent-memory systems preserve history through soft revocation: a contradicted fact is marked invalid and retained rather than deleted.   • Limitations and uncertainty handling — pages 9, 16

    The paper acknowledges that the rule-carrying and no-rule scenario groups are not a controlled comparison and that the matched-pair test has limited power to detect systematic differences.

    Attributed source statement: supported. The per-scenario analysis states the two groups differ in scenario, action set, and distractor count, and the study never measures the same scenario with and without its prohibition. The matched-pair appendix states that with 17 discordant pairs the test could not have detected a systematic advantage unless roughly three-quarters of discordant cells fell the same way, so the claim is only that the systems do not differ grossly. Claim/evidence check: supported.

    Across those four scenarios, the effect is largest where the unsafe action is operationally plausible and smallest where it is recognizable as harmful independently of policy: 3.9% for an over-limit transfer, 0.6% for waiving MFA, and no unsafe trial for log deletion.
