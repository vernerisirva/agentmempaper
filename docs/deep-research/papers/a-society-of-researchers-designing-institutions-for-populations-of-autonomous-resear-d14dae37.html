Quality pending

The manuscript introduces a compelling institutional perspective and clear design principles for scaling multi-agent research. However, because it lacks controlled comparative evaluations against established agent baselines and relies primarily on anecdotal output from an initial deployment, its scientific conclusions cannot be definitively validated at this stage.

The authors transparently acknowledge significant limitations and open challenges, including the absence of controlled baselines under matched compute (C1), uncalibrated judge personas (C2), vulnerability to proposal inflation (C4), and security risks such as compute-layer data leakage (C5).

Publication: preprint. arXiv manuscript identifier; no external publication established by the available metadata.
